The Algorithm Map

Sixteen Google updates, drawn as five transit lines. Each line is a question Google kept asking: is it good, is it trusted, does it work, does it understand me, can I rely on it? Tap a station, then audit a real site against the whole map.

1 · Read the map

Stations run left to right in time. Tap one to see what it changed and what to practise. Tick boxes in the audit lab below and exposed stations light up pink.

2 · Audit lab

Open a real website (your own, a client's, or a competitor's). Tick every statement that is true for it. You're finding the updates it would be exposed to.

    3 · Three classroom exercises

    Exercise A · 20 min

    Audit & rank

    Pairs audit the same site, compare ticks, and agree on the three costliest risks. Defend the order using the map.

    Exercise B · 15 min

    Predict, then run

    Pick a station, predict which results will rise or fall, then open it in the simulator and check your prediction.

    Exercise C · homework

    Fix & re-audit

    Apply two fixes to a practice site, re-run the audit, and write 150 words on which risk was hardest to remove, and why.

    Educational simplification: real updates blend many signals and Google rarely confirms causes. Treat each station as a lesson, not a formula. Dates refer to the main rollout of each update.
