Treat that statement as a question to investigate, not proof of a penalty or a prediction of rankings. Review representative URLs, compare pages with different purposes, and record concrete examples. Separate technical symptoms, such as duplicate URL versions, from content concerns such as copied or unhelpful pages.

A practical starting point is to use a consistent brand identity, About page, and credible mentions. Record the issue, prioritise changes that help visitors, and monitor crawling, indexing, and search performance afterwards.
